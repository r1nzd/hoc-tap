  2. The focus timer runs in your browser and requires no sign-up or account. Each session consists of 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break. The timer only runs while the page is open; if you close or reload the page, the timer stops and resets. The displayed time may be off by a few seconds when your browser suspends background tabs, and the timer does not send a notification when time is up if you are on another tab. The timer requires a browser with JavaScript enabled.
